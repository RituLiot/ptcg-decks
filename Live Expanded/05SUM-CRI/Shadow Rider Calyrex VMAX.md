# Shadow Rider Calyrex VMAX

**Competitiveness:** B | **Author:** Strategic TCG | **Source:** [YouTube video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vv1L6H8kFwg)

![decklist](../../!Images/Live%20Expanded/5SUM-CRI/Shadow%20Rider%20Calyrex%20VMAX.png)

## Thoughts
This deck is decently powerful. Early disruption attacks of *Chimecho* and *Frillish* buy you time to setup, and when you do you deal pretty good damage with *Shadow Rider Calyrex*. Only problem being - you need a lot of resources, and if you yourself are getting disrupted, then you might just run out of time before your opponent starts swinging. I definitely think this list could be improven to be more powerful.
